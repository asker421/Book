# English Translation Protocol — V4

Source of truth: `main/chapters_v4/`.

## Mandatory method

1. Translate from the current Russian source only.
2. Process strictly in source order: read one source line/paragraph, translate that unit, then move to the next.
3. Preserve every factual statement, action, reaction, temporal relation, reveal, and ambiguity.
4. Do not add explanations, worldbuilding, motives, sensory details, or connective material absent from the source.
5. Do not omit or merge information.
6. English syntax may be natural, but the informational content of each source unit must remain 1:1.
7. Preserve paragraph order and dialogue-turn order.
8. Character names and recurring terminology must remain consistent across the entire novel.
9. Old English translations are not a translation source and must not be copied from.
10. Before marking a chapter complete, compare the English text against the Russian source unit-by-unit.
