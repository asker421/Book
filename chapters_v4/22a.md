Interlude IV. In the Margins of the Record

The engineer turned his panel toward Mirena. A strip ran across it—the readings from every booth instrument during the minutes when Asgar first closed the door. The engineer had pulled up that strip to answer Asgar's question about the man in the corridor, and now he was staying with it: closer to the beginning were several measurements that had never been explained in the earlier analysis.

Mirena had not yet cleared the finished conversation from her own field. Asgar had received the answer and put down the receiver; there was no reason to call him again.

"Here," the engineer said. "Before the door closed."

Mirena found the faint external deviation. Beside it was the old note: physical interpretation not established. She opened the attached voice recording. Asgar was talking about the man who had come out before him; the woman on the line was asking him to confirm it. Mirena stopped playback when the engineer turned his result toward her.

"Look at it without the conversation first."

On his panel, the early marker stood among several others. Before, they had fallen into different groups; now processing with the original model collected some of them together. Above them was a label: "Connected to return history."

Mirena selected the early signal and opened the previous processing. The same faint series. It had not become any better. But now it could be followed to other events that had previously been considered separately.

"What links them?"

The engineer showed the explanation. Mirena began reading and asked him to expand the basis for one of the transitions. He did, then another. The farther they went, the less the label resembled a finished answer: the classifier was accounting for a set of dependencies the engineer could not yet translate into a clear physical statement.

"What if we remove the first trace?" Mirena asked.

He looked at her.

"From the check. So the others aren't being fitted to it."

The engineer excluded the early segment and ran the processing again. Mirena returned to her own console to check the module's state. There was no new call. She cleared the finished conversation from the upper field, freeing it for the next data, and returned to the engineer before the result arrived.

The group remained. When he added the early record back, it again fell among the similar events.

Mirena placed the old and new results side by side. Before this check, the early trace could have been set aside while they dealt with what began after the door closed. Now they had to return to it together with the rest. She slid the old note toward the engineer.

"This needs to be expanded."

"I still haven't established the cause."

"That's what it says. But now it isn't alone."

The engineer changed the tolerances. Some markers dropped out of the group; several remained. Mirena watched the early one: in this check it held. The engineer saved both variants and began preparing the materials for independent processing. The set included the raw series, the previous result, and the new model. Asgar's account remained as a separate attachment.

"If the check confirms the group, what do we tell him?" Mirena asked.

"That we found similar registrations. I can't say anything about the man from those."

She opened the recording of the previous conversation and found the place where he had spoken about her shift. She listened to those few seconds twice.

"He was wrong," the engineer said without turning around.

"He was."

"But you're listening."

"I'm checking whether I answered him worse than I remember."

Mirena closed the recording. She had answered exactly as she remembered, and that did not make it easier.

She asked him to send the result to her as well. The engineer added her to the recipients, then returned to the beginning of the sequence. The marker for the first door closure slid beyond the right edge of the enlarged section.

Mirena lingered beside him. Before, she had opened this part for the voice of a person who did not yet know what had happened to him. Now the engineer was checking what had been registered before him. She returned to her line, leaving the early segment open on the neighboring panel.
